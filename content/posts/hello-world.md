Welcome to my digital laboratory. I go by **Nickye**.

For a long time, I've been fascinated by the offensive side of security—understanding how systems are built so I can understand how they break. I have a background in infrastructure and software development, but when it comes to practical pentesting and bug bounty, I am starting from the ground up.

This blog is my public notebook. 

Instead of waiting until I am an "expert" to share knowledge, I decided to document the exact process of going from zero to hero in cybersecurity. You will see my lab setups, my write-ups on TryHackMe and Hack The Box, the tools I'm learning (like nmap and Burp Suite), and all the inevitable mistakes along the way.

If you are also navigating the overwhelming ocean of InfoSec, stick around. We are going to learn this together.
